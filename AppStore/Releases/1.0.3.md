# Blip 1.0.3 (6)

## English (U.S.)

Fixed keyboard switching on iPad. The globe button is now at the bottom left of the Blip keyboard. Tap it to switch keyboards or hold it to choose a keyboard.

## German

Der Tastaturwechsel auf dem iPad wurde korrigiert. Die Globus-Taste befindet sich jetzt unten links in der Blip-Tastatur. Antippen wechselt die Tastatur; gedrückt halten öffnet die Tastaturauswahl.

## Validation

- iPad (A16) simulator, iOS 26.5: tap and hold switching verified; bottom-left placement verified in letters, numeric, and large-number layouts.
- User inspected the simulator and confirmed the fix.
- Existing pending-scan insertion regression checks passed.
- Customer iPad hardware and iPadOS 27.0.1 have not been tested.
- Submission state will be recorded after App Store Connect confirms it.
